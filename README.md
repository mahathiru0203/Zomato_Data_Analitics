# Zomato Data Analytics Project

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing **Zomato restaurant data** to uncover insights into customer preferences, restaurant performance, and food trends. The goal is to leverage data analytics techniques to understand dining patterns and provide actionable recommendations for businesses in the food and hospitality industry.

## Objectives
- Explore restaurant distribution across cities and cuisines.
- Identify factors influencing restaurant ratings and popularity.
- Analyze cost trends and affordability across regions.
- Understand customer preferences based on cuisine, location, and price.
- Provide insights for strategic decision-making in the food industry.

## Dataset
- **Source:** Zomato restaurant dataset (CSV/Excel format).
- **Features include:**
  - Restaurant name, location, and type
  - Cuisine categories
  - Average cost for two
  - Ratings and votes
  - City and country information

## Tools & Technologies
- **Python** 
- **Libraries:**
  - `pandas` – data cleaning & manipulation
  - `numpy` – numerical analysis
  - `matplotlib` & `seaborn` – data visualization
- **Power BI / Tableau** – interactive dashboards (optional)

## Key Insights
- Popular cuisines and their distribution across cities.
- Relationship between cost and ratings.
- City-wise restaurant density and performance.
- Customer voting trends and their impact on ratings.
